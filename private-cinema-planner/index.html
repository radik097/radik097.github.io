Calculation method, limitations and sources

This page uses the figures from the user's original table. For groups smaller than five, the recommended food budget is recalculated proportionally per guest:

  • Henkel Street Cinema: $129 hire + approximately $23 of food per guest.
  • Village Cinemas Vsuite: $350 hire + approximately $30 of food per guest.
  • FOMO Cinemas: $500–$650 hire + approximately $35 of food per guest.

The FOMO range is shown as a minimum and maximum total. All amounts are in Australian dollars.
